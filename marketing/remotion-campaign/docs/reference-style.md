# Reference Style

Reference URL:

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XgM2_m2I6sE?feature=share

## What To Borrow

- Vertical app promo structure.
- Large iPhone mockup as the hero.
- Multiple phone frames/screens moving smoothly.
- Clear product-demo feel.
- Dynamic slides, zooms, and screen swaps.
- Short captions around the phone.

## What Not To Copy

- Do not copy exact colors, typography, layout, text, or branding.
- Do not copy any music, voiceover, or copyrighted visual material.
- Do not use fake App Store badges.

## TeaTimer Translation

- Warm tea colors: cream, green, soft gold, tea brown.
- Premium minimal iPhone app launch mood.
- Real TeaTimer-inspired dark UI, tea categories, scanner flow, saved teas, timer, and review screens.
- Generated tea photos remain background atmosphere only.
- The app UI inside the iPhone is the main focus.
